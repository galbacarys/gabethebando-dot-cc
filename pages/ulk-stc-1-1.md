Mottled half-light filters through still shaggy deciduous trees. Green is their
age, green are the youngsters of the forest. They merrily dance in the breeze of
morning, their whispered rustling awakening a lone body in the darkened theater
of the peatmoss floor below. An incongruous "zip" is the only clear indication that
this creature is not _of_ the forest, but merely today's honored guest. A dew drop
lingers for just long enough to make its host leaf twinkle like impossible morning
starlight before falling rapturously onto a surprised forehead. Deep belly laughs:
the mirth of the leaves' endless dance gives life to the jewel of moisture brings
an unexpected joy to the visitor. Breath is drawn deeply into still-warming lungs.
A fire is stoked. Water darkened with coffee grounds bubbles in a dented tin vessel.
The forest is perceived.
